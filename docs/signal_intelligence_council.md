# Signal Intelligence Council

## Purpose

Andela places AI talent at a premium. Sustaining that premium depends on a reliable connection between market demand, learning design, credential integrity, placement outcomes, and commercial positioning.

The Signal Intelligence Council owns that connection for the Decision Spine. It maintains the evidence base, governs the quality standard, and ensures the AI talent pipeline stays aligned with where the market is heading.

## Role In The Decision Spine

The council is the accountable governance layer for the system:

```text
market signal -> evidence judgment -> credential requirement -> learning change -> placement outcome -> prediction learning
```

The council does not simply report market movement. It decides which evidence is strong enough to shape curriculum, credentialing, matching, and commercial positioning.

## Operating Scope

### 1. Market Intelligence And Horizon Architecture

The council monitors the AI engineering labour market and anticipates shifts in the broader AI landscape, translating demand signals and emerging patterns into inputs for learning design, talent positioning, and commercial strategy.

Core responsibilities:

- Apply Andela's AI Talent Framework as the classification lens for programme design, talent positioning, and market intelligence.
- Evolve the AI Talent Framework using market evidence and placement outcome data.
- Operate a market intelligence pipeline that tracks emerging skill demands, shifting role patterns, and timing signals for curriculum response.
- Produce horizon reviews and signal briefs with defined internal distribution.
- Translate market intelligence into credential requirements and learning outcome recommendations for Assessment Ops and Developer Learning.
- Advise cross-functional stakeholders on evidence-based AI talent positioning.
- Represent Andela's AI talent development methodology externally through publication, speaking, and market discourse.

### 2. Evidence And Integration

The council provides the evidence base that commercial, matching, and learning functions depend on to position and develop AI talent effectively.

Core responsibilities:

- Keep the Prototyper / Builder / Scaler archetype affinity model aligned with market demand.
- Ensure tagging criteria reflect demonstrated capability, not aspirational positioning.
- Evaluate content and vendor selections for alignment with credentialing standards and learning outcomes.
- Provide evidence for commercial positioning and rate premium authorization for AI-certified talent.
- Track which learning signals predict placement success, client satisfaction, and retention.
- Feed placement and client outcome findings back into programme design.

## Decision Rights

| Area | Council Authority |

## Council Capabilities

- Workforce development, learning system design, or talent strategy in a technology context.
- Translation of labour market evidence into learning or credentialing decisions.
- Assessment framework, credential model, and quality assurance governance.
- Cross-functional operating authority across commercial, product, and operational teams.
- Working knowledge of the AI engineering landscape, including current tooling, workflows, role patterns, and pace of change.
- Market intelligence and horizon scanning methods.
- Pedagogical design frameworks, including competency-based design, deliberate practice, and formative and summative assessment.
- Clear communication across technical and non-technical audiences.
- Judgment to distinguish durable signal from market noise.
